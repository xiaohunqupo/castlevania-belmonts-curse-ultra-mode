功能更新版 / Feature release.

## 简体中文

- **快速施法**：不用切换装备，按组合键直接释放已获得的塔罗牌法术，消耗按那张牌自己的计算。默认：
  - 上 + Y：时空飞冲
  - 下 + Y：地狱火葬
  - 按住 LT + Y：彩绘玻璃护盾（和弹反同时生效）
  - R3：赦免（还没有赦免时为诅咒之火）
  - 按住 RB + Y：石化凝视
  - 按住 RB + 下 + Y：塔罗斯之拳
  - 按住 RB + R3：灵魂收割
  - 赦免、灵魂收割这类需要按住的法术，按住组合键即可蓄力。
  - 每张牌用哪个组合键，在 MODS 页签新增的"奥创模式 - 快速施法"子页里选，开关和恢复默认也在这一页（也可以改配置文件 `07 Tarot`），没获得的牌按原版处理。
- **装备塔罗牌页面的快速施法说明**：有快捷键的牌，描述末尾显示"按下/按住 组合键 快速施法"，按键用游戏自己的图标。
- **法术优化子页**：MODS 页签新增"奥创模式 - 法术优化"，集中放单张法术的改动。
- **时空飞冲无敌**：从起跳到落地全程无敌，陷阱、即死区域、坠落伤害仍然有效。
- **圣十字架撞墙不消失**：撞墙继续弹射，回到身边时仍按原版回收。
- **十字键快捷换装**：左右切换各类型已获得的最高级武器，上下切换未设置快速施法的法术；攻击中会等到动作结束或可连段间隙再换，避免打断动画；十字键不再用于移动。
- **升级回满**：获得经验升级时 HP、MP 回满。
- **经验雕像保证升级**：击碎经验雕像后，掉落的经验不够升一级时自动补足。
- **钩锁辅助**：空中、爬墙后下落时，附近明明有钩点，按钩锁却没反应，已修复。
- **「没有可钩目标时禁用钩锁」默认关闭**（设置页「奥创模式」里，钩锁速度下方）：附近没有可钩物时按钩锁仍会按原版挥出，避免被当成钩锁失灵；需要时仍可打开。已经有配置文件的玩家不会自动改，可在设置页关掉该项。
- **攻击加速同步修复**：加快前摇/后摇时，命中框、蓄力门槛以前没有一起缩放，和位移/取消/松手窗口错位，会出现点按不出招、连点鞭子卡在起手、打中却无伤或第二下 miss。现在命中框与蓄力台阶会随倍率一起缩放。感谢 Nexus 上 **esco1979**、**AstralRaven07** 的详细反馈。
- 另感谢 **Vaatu8** 报告的特定房间掉帧、以及与 BepInEx Configuration Manager / 游戏更新相关的启动问题；这两项仍在排查，尚未在本版一并修好。

**下载哪个**：

- `UltraMode-v0.2.0-with-BepInEx.zip`：**整合包**，内含官方原版 BepInEx 6 IL2CPP x64 be.788。没装过 BepInEx 的选这个，解压到游戏根目录即可；第一次启动需要几分钟，请保持联网。
- `UltraMode-v0.2.0.zip`：只有本 mod。已经装了旧版本的玩家下载这个，覆盖 `BepInEx/plugins/UltraMode/` 即可，配置会保留，新增的选项会自动加上。

## English

- **Quick Cast**: cast any tarot spell you own with a button combo, without equipping it; it costs what that card costs. Defaults:
  - Up + Y: Chronomantic Dive
  - Down + Y: Infernal Pyre
  - Hold LT + Y: Stained-Glass Shield (together with the parry)
  - R3: Absolution (Cursed Flame until you have Absolution)
  - Hold RB + Y: Petrifying Gaze
  - Hold RB + Down + Y: Fist of Talos
  - Hold RB + R3: Soul Harvest
  - Held spells such as Absolution and Soul Harvest charge while the combo is held.
  - Pick the combo of each card on the new "Ultra Mode - Quick Cast" page of the MODS tab, which also has the on/off switch and its own reset (or in the config file's `07 Tarot` section); cards you don't own behave as in vanilla.
- **Quick Cast line on the tarot equipment page**: cards with a shortcut end their description with "Press/Hold *combo* to quick cast", using the game's own button icons.
- **Spell Tweaks page**: a new "Ultra Mode - Spell Tweaks" page on the MODS tab for per-spell changes.
- **Invulnerable Chronomantic Dive**: no damage from takeoff to landing. Traps, death zones and fall damage still apply.
- **Holy Cross survives walls**: keeps bouncing off walls; catch-back is unchanged.
- **D-pad quick equip**: left/right cycles the best owned weapon of each type; up/down cycles spells without a Quick Cast binding; swaps wait until the attack ends or reaches its chainable gap so mid-swing equips do not break the animation; the D-pad no longer moves.
- **Refill on level up**: leveling up from experience restores all HP and MP.
- **Statues always level up**: when a broken experience statue drops less experience than the next level needs, the rest is added.
- **Whip assist**: fixed the whip button doing nothing in the air or when dropping off a wall although an anchor was in reach.
- **"Block whip with nothing to hook" is off by default** (Ultra Mode page, under whip speed): with nothing to hook, the whip still throws as in vanilla, so a miss is not mistaken for the whip being broken; turn the option on if you want the old block. An existing config file keeps its value; switch it off on the settings page if needed.
- **Attack speed sync fix**: when wind-up/recovery were sped up, hurtboxes and charge thresholds were not scaled with movement/cancel/release windows, which could make taps fail to attack, whip mashing stutter on startup, or hits push/miss with no damage. Hurtboxes and charge steps now scale with the multipliers. Thanks to **esco1979** and **AstralRaven07** on Nexus for the detailed reports.
- Also thanks to **Vaatu8** for reporting a room-specific FPS drop and a launch issue with BepInEx Configuration Manager / a game update; those are still under investigation and are not fixed in this release.

**Which file**:

- `UltraMode-v0.2.0-with-BepInEx.zip`: **all-in-one**, includes the official BepInEx 6 IL2CPP x64 be.788. Pick this if you have never installed BepInEx; unzip into the game folder. The first launch takes a few minutes and needs an internet connection.
- `UltraMode-v0.2.0.zip`: this mod only. If you have an older version, download this and overwrite `BepInEx/plugins/UltraMode/`; your settings are kept and the new options are added.
