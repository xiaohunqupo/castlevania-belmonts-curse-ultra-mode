问题修复版 / Bug-fix release.

## 简体中文

- **修复镜之厅最上层的崩溃**：走到镜之厅最上层那面镜子前游戏会直接崩溃（关掉奥创模式则不会）。原因是"修复破碎镜子"给游戏判断镜子能否使用的方法打了补丁，这个方法太短，补丁破坏了它的内部跳转。现在不再给它打补丁，存档室的破镜子改为只靠换回原来的完好镜子来修复。
- **"修复破碎镜子"只处理存档室**：镜之厅中枢里按目的地分组的镜子不再被替换；关闭这个开关或关闭奥创模式时，已经替换的镜子当场还原。
- **钩锁辅助**：
  - 附近没有可钩的东西时偶尔仍会挥出钩锁，已修复。
  - 只有一个勾点高亮时偶尔需要按方向才能钩中，已修复：现在会一直锁定瞄准它，直到这次钩锁结束。
  - 钩锁被打断后辅助偶尔整体失效，已修复。
- **镜子提示**：某面镜子的检查出错时只跳过这一面，不再影响其他镜子。

**下载哪个**：

- `UltraMode-v0.1.1-with-BepInEx.zip`：**整合包**，内含官方原版 BepInEx 6 IL2CPP x64 be.788。没装过 BepInEx 的选这个，解压到游戏根目录即可；第一次启动需要几分钟，请保持联网。
- `UltraMode-v0.1.1.zip`：只有本 mod。已经装了 v0.1.0 的玩家下载这个，覆盖 `BepInEx/plugins/UltraMode/` 即可，配置会保留。

## English

- **Fixed a crash at the top of the Hall of Mirrors**: walking up to the topmost mirror crashed the game (it did not with Ultra Mode off). "Repair broken mirrors" patched the game's mirror-usable check, a function too short for the patch, which broke a jump inside it. That method is no longer patched; broken save room mirrors are repaired only by bringing back the original intact mirror.
- **"Repair broken mirrors" only touches save rooms**: the destination mirror groups in the Hall of Mirrors hub are left alone, and swapped mirrors are restored at once when the option or Ultra Mode is turned off.
- **Whip assist**:
  - Fixed the whip sometimes still being thrown with nothing to hook.
  - Fixed a lone highlighted anchor sometimes needing a direction to be hooked: the aim now stays locked on it until the whip ends.
  - Fixed the assist sometimes stopping altogether after an interrupted whip.
- **Mirror prompt**: a mirror whose check fails is skipped on its own instead of affecting the others.

**Which file**:

- `UltraMode-v0.1.1-with-BepInEx.zip`: **all-in-one**, includes the official BepInEx 6 IL2CPP x64 be.788. Pick this if you have never installed BepInEx; unzip into the game folder. The first launch takes a few minutes and needs an internet connection.
- `UltraMode-v0.1.1.zip`: this mod only. If you already have v0.1.0, download this and overwrite `BepInEx/plugins/UltraMode/`; your settings are kept.
